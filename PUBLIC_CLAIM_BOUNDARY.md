# Public Claim Boundary

This boundary applies to the revised manuscript and companion repository as of
2026-09-20.

## Can Say

- The eight historical congruent dissections are reconstructed with the
  stated exact atlas and refinement checks. Their Dehn values follow from
  additivity and congruence.
- The Lagrange, Burnside, 24-cell, and S6 -> S12 statements hold within the
  scopes written in the paper.
- The n=16 all-one-face result is conditional on its two hypotheses and
  unconditional in the Coxeter-pure instance.
- Under BRH, five congruent convex pieces cannot partition the regular
  tetrahedron (`thm:n5`).
- Exact common-role area bookkeeping has the six listed positive multisets,
  and the NN polynomial identity holds in the named local chart.

## Must Not Say

- That congruence alone implies BRH or a common exposed-facet set.
- That arbitrary five-piece congruent dissections are impossible.
- That the old phase-26 stack reduces the unrestricted n=5 problem to one
  accidental-even residual. That statement is retired.
- That an area-incidence matrix is a geometric dissection.
- That the NN identity covers all class-6 branches or mixed boundary roles.
- That any replay is an independent specialist review or formal verification.
- That every individual dihedral angle is alpha, beta, or a rational multiple
  of pi. That stronger classification is withdrawn; the total Dehn values
  do not require it.
- That n | 24 holds for all congruent dissections; use the scope in C2.

## Scope Notes

All active certificates use exact rational or symbolic arithmetic. The active
n=5 theorem is a written conditional argument; the scripts check its finite
arithmetic and symmetry components but cannot establish its geometric
hypothesis for an arbitrary partition.
